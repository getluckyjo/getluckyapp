Get Lucky Golf wants VALR's rails to take entry fees in the golfer's own currency in South Africa, Europe, the US and Korea, and to pay hole-in-one winners anywhere in minutes. A legal, skill-based challenge: the golfer's own shot decides it.

What Get Lucky is

A skill-based hole-in-one challenge in an app. A golfer picks a par 3, pays an entry fee of R50 to R1,000, records one tee shot on their phone, and if the ball goes in they win up to R1,000,000. There is no draw, no random element and no odds we can move: the hole is fixed and shown before anyone pays, and only the golfer's shot decides it. Every prize is insured by Santam, every winning shot is video-verified before a cent moves, and players are 18+. It is the hole-in-one challenge South African golf days have run legally for decades. Ernie Els is coming on as founding ambassador, and our Golfzon integration puts the challenge on 15,000 golf simulators around the world.

What we need

  • Take entry fees in the local currency. A golfer on a Golfzon simulator in Seoul pays in won, in Dallas in dollars, in Munich in euros. Today we can only take rand.
  • Pay winners anywhere, fast. A verified winner gets the prize in minutes, not a SWIFT transfer, a week's wait and a bank's cut.
  • A rail that understands a skill challenge. Card acquirers' merchant categories lump an entry fee with an insured prize in with games of chance, whatever the skill or the insurance behind it. Our wallet processor declined us on that basis this month. PayFast carries rand entry fees and nothing else.
  • Hold prizes in dollars. A $50,000 prize should be a $50,000 prize whatever the rand does.

What VALR can help with

  • Local-currency entry fees abroad. Golfers in Europe, the US and Korea pay in EUR, USD and KRW through VALR's fiat and stablecoin on-ramps, settled to us in USDC or USDT.
  • Stablecoin payouts. Winners paid in USDC or USDT to a VALR wallet within the hour, with local currency a tap away.
  • Pay with VALR. A one-tap entry for VALR's users in rand, USDT or USDC, and a rand rail on VALR's payment licence for everyone else.
  • Prize treasury. Our dollar float held in USDC on VALR Business, converted at payout, automated through the API.
  • A broadcast moment. A VALR hole-in-one prize paid live, on air, in crypto, in December.

What we would like from a first meeting

  • VALR's compliance view, early. We are a legal, skill-based challenge with an insured prize, not a game of chance; we would rather hear where VALR lands in the first meeting than the last.
  • Which of the above can be live for December, and which is a 2027 build.
  • How a winner abroad gets paid under the draft exchange-control rules, and what VALR needs from us to scope a pilot.
