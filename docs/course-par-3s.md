# The par 3s, course by course

Every course in the app, its par 3s as the app offers them after
migration 035, the tee the distance is from, and where it was checked.
Checked in October 2026 after Johannes found Atlantic Beach's 12th offered
as a par 3 (the course's nines were swapped years ago; it is the 3rd now).

The app keeps one distance per hole, in metres, from the club's standard
men's tee: white at most clubs, yellow where that is the members' tee.
Hole eligibility is a par 3 of 140 m or more (`src/lib/holes.ts`), so the
tee matters near that line; a day played from other tees changes the hole
in Admin → Courses. Sources: the club's own card, hole guide or course
page where one exists online, otherwise GolfPass, 18Birdies, Golfify or
Hole19, whose cards were cross-checked against each other and against
SA Top 100's hole-by-hole reviews. GolfPass lists yards; they were
converted (× 0.9144), which is how migration 019's distances went wrong
in the first place (it stored the yards as metres).

**What 035 changed.** Twenty-seven courses had at least one hole that is
not a par 3; those holes are switched off and the real par 3s added.
Distances more than 10 m out from the club's men's tee are corrected;
smaller differences are left. The eight Cape Town courses migration 034
settled are untouched. Seven courses keep their distances although the
cards differ, because the evidence is weak: Blair Atholl (the club's hole
guide is not labelled by tee), Emfuleni (three cards disagree; the app is
within 7 m of the white tee), Magalies Park (being re-teed in 2025/26),
Wedgewood (club site down, third-party cards disagree), Wingate Park
(two club cards disagree), Pinnacle Point (club page and GolfPass
disagree on 9 and 17) and Randpark Firethorn (the app carries the
yellow tee consistently; the club tee is 9–13 m shorter).

**Worth a second look.** Mossel Bay (no club card online; the app now
carries the yellow tee, 141/152/192/134; the white is 123/133/171/118,
which would leave only the 12th playable), Nelspruit (now Mbombela Golf
Club; the club tee Blue Wood is used, the White Stinkwood is 210/140/
170/120), Paarl (27 holes: the app carries the Old Course, holes 1–18;
the Boschenmeer nine's par 3s are its 2nd and 5th at 145/169 m), Hermanus
(27 holes: the app carries the East course, 1–18) and Metropolitan (the
club's hole guide, used by 034, is 14–24 m shorter than 18Birdies' card).
